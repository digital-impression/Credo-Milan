Credo / 01

Credo

[ˈkreeːdoː]

Latin for ‘I believe’

We believe in an approach that starts from you.

Your body, your goal and your question are the starting point. From there we look broadly at what is needed to make progress. With involvement, knowledge based on the latest science and a goal-driven approach, we bring you step by step closer to your goal.

Value 01 Motivation +

Progress you can see in black and white. We record a baseline, set a goal beside it, and show you at every retest what has actually shifted.

Value 02 Passion +

The method you find here in the gym is the same one we use on the pitch at professional clubs. Because that is where we hold it up to the light every week.

Value 03 Empathy +

Your ceiling is rarely where you think it is. We first listen to what your sport, your schedule and your body can handle, and build the programme around that, not the other way round.

What we offer / 02

Getting fitter
is a method.

Getting fitter means working specifically on the qualities that make the difference for you. With the right method we build, step by step, the physical base you need to reach your goals.

Screening

Screening
& testing

We start by understanding where you stand today. Through targeted screening and testing we map out your body, your movement quality and your physical capacity.

That is how we find where the opportunities are and what you need to make real progress.

See the programme

Train

Functional training
& performance

No fixed training package, but an approach that starts from what you need. Depending on your goal and your starting point we use the right forms of training, from strength work and stability to HIIT and sport-specific training.

We adjust where needed and also provide the right support outside the practice, with personal schedules and advice for at home.

How a block runs

Goal

Pushing your limits

The end goal? To be able to do more than today. A body strong, fit and resilient enough to keep up with your ambitions, your challenges and your life.

So that you are defined not by what your body can do today, but by what you want to achieve tomorrow.

Still in rehabilitation? Start here

Lifestyle

Lifestyle

From general health advice to marginal gains for elite athletes. Health and performance are determined by more than training alone.

That is why we look at every factor that affects how you feel, recover and function. From nutrition and sleep to stress, recovery and daily habits. We listen to your challenges and look for concrete solutions that fit your goals.

Programme / 03

How we build you up

  1. Step 01

    Appointment

    You can get an appointment quickly, so we can start straight away.

  2. Step 02

    Screening & goal setting

    We map your starting point precisely with objective testing and technology, and set down together where you want to go.

  3. Step 03

    Programme

    Based on your goals and your screening we build a focused programme that grows with you.

  4. Step 04

    Train

    Train with purpose, execute correctly and keep adjusting. That is how we get the most out of every session.

  5. Step 05

    Retest

    We test again, compare the results and map your progress objectively.

Practical / 09

Before you call.

What does a screening cost? +

A full screening takes 60 minutes and costs €72.

How often per week? +

We work flexibly and offer everyone an individual approach. Whether you need a personal training schedule to complete at home or prefer individual guidance during your sessions at our practice, we adapt our support to your goals and your needs.

Can this run alongside my club training? +

Our guidance fits perfectly alongside your club training and is often a valuable addition. We take the training load within your club into account and have wide experience in monitoring and managing fatigue.

What ages is this suitable for? +

Our guidance suits every age, young and old, with an approach tailored to each person’s goals and possibilities.

How long does a programme take? +

A training cycle lasts six to eight weeks, after which you can request a new screening and evaluate your progress. That gives enough room for progression and lets us adjust in good time where needed.

Do I get my numbers? +

We objectify your data and translate it into a clear, readable report that you can consult at home as well.
